Click any column header to sort; click a book for its holding-by-holding breakdown. Indices (dashed) are USD total return from the common start 2026-06-03. L1M is the trailing one-month return, shown only once a book has a full month of history (n/a before that); an L3M column joins it once the field reaches three months. Sharpe is annualised reward-to-variability of daily USD-TR returns (√252×mean/σ, rf=0); paused until a book has 30 daily returns (~six weeks), below which the annualised figure is mostly noise. JAOD uses the same uniform-weight field method as everyone else (the official whole-share book differs slightly).
What is each book actually expressing?
Deterministic factor exposures — each book's daily USD total-return moves correlated against tradeable index funds (listed in the methodology at the bottom). No estimation of intent — just how the book has co-moved. Correlation is direction-and-strength (−1 to +1); a rate row shows TLT co-movement, so positive = benefits from falling rates (long duration).
Cells shade green for positive co-movement, red for negative. Click any column header to sort.

Click any holding row to break its contribution into stock price vs dividends, and decompose the price move (multiple vs fundamentals vs shares). Weight is the holding's live share of book value. Price is the latest close; VWAP your average cost per share. Ret 1M / 1Y are the stock's trailing price returns (market context); Vol 1Y is its trailing-1-year annualised return volatility (√252×σ of daily returns); Ret today the latest close-to-close move; Ret ITD your position's total return (price + dividends) over capital deployed. Contrib today / ITD are that name's P&L as a % of the $100k starting book (ITD folds in dividends), so each column sums to the book total. Cash earns the money-market rate (BIL), shown as its own line. The Total row's Ret today / ITD are measured against the $100k starting book — so they tie the summed contributions exactly (the leaderboard's 1D uses current NAV, a hair larger when a book sits below $100k). Capture ↑/↓ (tiles above) is the book's average daily return on benchmark-up days / benchmark-down days as a % of the benchmark's own average — vs the S&P 500 (SPY) and Global (ACWI); above 100 on ↑ means it amplifies rallies, below 100 on ↓ means it cushions sell-offs. Appears once the book overlaps ≥5 up and ≥5 down benchmark days.

Inflows & outflows — the full ledger
Every cash event with a date: initial funding, each buy/sell at its trade price, and each dividend showing its ex-date (when the entitlement is fixed by shares held) vs its pay-date (when the cash is actually delivered). A dividend is a receivable carried in book value from the ex-date until it becomes spendable cash on the pay-date — recorded on the ex-date, then a separate cash-in entry on the pay-date. Most names use each issuer's real pay schedule where known (some preferreds pay month-end); otherwise a ~three-week lag is assumed.
